---
title: "Read a grade"
---

A grade is five numbers and the lines that produced them. The letter is a summary, not the
finding.

## Read it in this order

1. **The five scores, not the letter.** Two trades can both grade B for opposite reasons. The
   shape of the five is the finding: a B that lost 25 points on cost drag is a friction problem,
   a B that lost them on exit discipline is a discipline problem.
2. **The evidence line under each score.** Every component prints the number that produced it.
   `cost: 0.2262 USDT of friction against a gross of 0.0491 USDT, so friction was the whole trade`
   is the grade. The 0 out of 5 is just its shadow.
3. **The neutral lines.** `exit: nothing in the record measures this, so it scores a neutral 2.5`
   means the record had nothing to judge, not that the exit was average. Neutral never costs
   points and never earns them.
4. **The reasoning line.** `reasoning: the judge scored the stated thesis 2 out of 5` came from a
   model reading the trader's own note against four fixed questions. With no model key the line
   says the note was left ungraded and the score holds at 2.

## What the letter means

A at 85 and above, B at 70, C at 55, D at 40, E below, on the weighted total out of 100.

## What a grade is not

It is not a prediction and it is not a claim about luck. A profitable trade can grade D and a
losing trade can grade A, and both happen in the demo bundle: the worst-graded trip in it, C at
60.9, is the one whose costs ate the gross.

Profit and loss is shown as a fact about the trade. It is never an input to the first four scores.
