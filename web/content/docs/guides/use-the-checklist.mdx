---
title: "Use the checklist"
---

The checklist is what the patterns became. One pattern that fired, one item, with a test name the
gate switches on. Nothing on it was chosen by hand, which is why it is short: the bundle on the
desk earned exactly one item.

## The map from pattern to item

| Pattern | Item | Test |
|---|---|---|
| `revenge-trading` | No re-entry within 30 minutes of a loss in the same name | `no-reentry-after-loss` |
| `weekend-overexposure` | Net exposure into a weekend stays under 40 percent of equity | `weekend-exposure-under-40` |
| `chasing-the-gap` | No entries while the price is more than 1 percent from the last regular close | `divergence-under-1pct` |
| `overtrading` | This week of turnover stays under 5 times equity | `weekly-turnover-under-5x` |
| `holding-losers` | No adding to a name that is already a day old and under water | `no-stale-loser-open` |
| `concentration` | No instrument holds more than half of open exposure | `symbol-under-half-exposure` |
| `fee-bleed` | The round trip costs less than 20 basis points, a fifth of a one percent move | `cost-under-20-bps` |
| `ignored-stops` | No open position in this name is already past its stop | `stop-not-already-crossed` |

The ninth detector, `added-while-under-water`, has no automatic test of its own. A pattern with no
test still becomes an item, marked `by-hand` for a person to check, because dropping it would
quietly shrink the checklist the review just earned.

## Reading an item

Each item carries a stable id (`check-` and the pattern name), the pattern it came from, the
sentence a person reads, and the test the gate runs. The id is stable across reviews, so a note
kept against an item survives the next review.

## An empty checklist

No pattern fired means no item, and the desk says that in those words rather than showing a blank
page. It is a result about the record, not a gap in the product.

## Running it

The checklist does nothing on its own. [The gate](/docs/guides/check-an-idea) runs every item against the
live market before an order exists.
