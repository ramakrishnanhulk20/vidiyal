---
title: "Ask a question"
---

`/ask` answers from the review in front of you and shows its working. The mechanism is in
[the honesty check](/docs/concepts/the-honesty-check); this page is how to use it.

## Questions the retrieval understands

The filter looks for a symbol or its underlying (`RMUUSDT`, `RMU`, `MU`), a side (`long`,
`short`), a grade letter (`grade C`), the words of a pattern that fired (`fee bleed`), or a word
about time (`today`, `yesterday`, `week`, `overnight`, `weekend`).

The page offers suggestions built from the record itself: the trade that graded worst, the
patterns that actually fired, the sessions these entries happened in. A question the desk cannot
answer from the bundle is never offered.

## When nothing matches

The five worst trades by total score answer instead. That is on purpose: a question asked of a
review is nearly always a question about what went wrong.

## Reading the answer

Under the narrative is the evidence table, which is the entire world the model was given. Each row
carries the round trip id it belongs to, so every sentence can be traced back to a trade and every
trade back to a signed record.

Under that are the cited references, and, when the check fired, the list of numbers the draft
carried that no row holds. From `npm run proof:review` on 2026-09-12:

```
  cited refs: ledger-demo:4>ledger-demo:6, ledger-demo:4>open
  numbers with no evidence behind them: none, every number in the answer came from a trade
```

## Limits worth knowing

A question is trimmed to 300 characters. The answer is drawn from at most five trades. With no
model key (`QWEN_API_KEY` first, `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY` as the fallback) the evidence table writes the paragraph itself, which is plainer English and
exactly as true. The engine run is given 90 seconds before the desk stops waiting and says so.
