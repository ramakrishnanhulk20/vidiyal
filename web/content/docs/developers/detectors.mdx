title: "The detectors"
label: "Detectors"
---

Nine, in `src/review/patterns.ts`, each exported on its own and run together by
`detectPatterns()`. Pure functions over the trade table: no network, no model. A detector with too
little to work on returns null rather than a maybe.

| Detector | Fires when | Thresholds in the code |
|---|---|---|
| `revenge-trading` | A new entry in the same instrument within 30 minutes of a losing exit, at a larger notional than the loser | 30 minutes |
| `weekend-overexposure` | Net exposure into a weekend above 40 percent of equity, or an unhedged rToken position above 10 percent of equity | 0.4 and 0.1 |
| `chasing-the-gap` | More than one entry while divergence from the last regular close was over 1 percent | 1 percent, at least 2 trades |
| `overtrading` | A week with turnover above 5 times equity where friction ate more than 30 percent of gross | 5 times, 0.3 |
| `holding-losers` | Median holding time of losers above twice that of winners | 2 times |
| `concentration` | One instrument above half of open exposure for more than a day | 0.5, 24 hours |
| `fee-bleed` | Fees plus slippage plus funding above 20 percent of gross over the period | 0.2 |
| `ignored-stops` | A position crossed its stop price and stayed open past the next tick | the stop in the record |
| `added-while-under-water` | A fill made a position bigger while the mark was worse than the position's average entry | the record's own marks |

Every threshold above is also written in `docs/rubric.md` in the repo, and the constants in
`patterns.ts` are the same numbers.

## Evidence, not claims

Each detector returns the round trip ids that prove it and the lines that fired it. The desk draws
a card from those, so a card without trades behind it cannot be produced.

## Three that need explaining

**`concentration`** walks the moments between entries and exits and keeps a running streak per
symbol. The walk ends at the last recorded entry or exit: a position still open past the end of
the record is not counted, because nothing in the record says it was still there.

**`added-while-under-water`** walks fills, not trips, because an add is a second entry into a
position that is still open and inside a single trip it can never be seen. It keeps a running size
and average entry per market the way the exchange does, so the third add is measured against the
average of the first two. The mark is the newest price the record observed at or before the fill,
and with no mark at all nothing is claimed. Its proof is carried back from fills to the trips that
hold them before anything reads it.

**`weekend-overexposure`** treats a SPOT symbol matching `R` plus two to six letters plus `USDT`
as a tokenized stock, and counts a position as hedged when a perpetual on the same underlying is
open at the same moment.

## Naming

`scripts/review.ts` takes the list of detectors that ran from the module's own exports and
hyphenates the camel case name, rather than keeping a written list that could fall behind the
code. That is what lets the desk say "these nine looked and found nothing" and mean it.
