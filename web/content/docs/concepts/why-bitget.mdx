---
title: "Why Bitget's tech is the heart"
label: "Why Bitget is the heart"
---

Take Bitget out of Vidiyal and there is no product left. Three things depend on it and none of
them has a substitute in this design.

## The record comes through Agent Hub

An account review reads real fills, real fees and a real balance through Agent Hub read verbs:
`order` fills 30 days per call across both product lines, `account_overview` for total equity now,
and `funds_records` financial rows for the balance changes the equity curve is walked back from.
That is the whole source of truth for a person's review. There is no upload, no CSV, no form to
type into, because a typed record is a memory and Vidiyal grades the record instead of the memory.

The reader is `src/ingest/account.ts` and it is read-only by construction: the SDK picks the
credentials out of the environment, `createBitget()` builds the tool surface read-only, and no
write verb is reachable from that file.

## The context comes from Bitget market data

A grade is only worth reading because the market at that moment is rebuilt around the trade.
Every one of those numbers is a Bitget public read:

- `candlesHistory` for the last regular close before the entry, the divergence at entry and exit,
  and whether a stop was crossed while the position was open.
- `orderbook` for the depth behind the cost estimate.
- `instruments` for quantity precision and the base coin, so a previewed order is sized the way
  Bitget wants it.
- `fundingRate` and `fundingRateHistory` for the funding a perpetual paid, charged into cost drag.
- `tickers` for the live price the gate sizes an idea against.

These need no key and no account, which is why a judge can run the proof command on a fresh
checkout and still get real numbers.

Tokenized stocks are the reason the review has anything interesting to say. Bitget lists them and
they trade around the clock while the US market is shut, so the price walks away from the last
regular close and re-anchors at the open. Divergence, the session clock and the 09:25 to 09:40 ET
re-anchoring window are Bitget-specific facts, and three of the nine detectors are about them.

## The gate ends at the Agent Hub dry run

A checklist that only prints advice is an opinion piece. This one ends at
`order` / `place` with `dryRun: true`, so a passing idea produces the exact request Bitget would
receive, and the SDK hands it back rather than sending it. The preview is built in its own
function against its own config, so the read-only context the rest of Vidiyal uses can never be
the one holding a write verb.

That is the line the product is built on: everything up to the order, nothing past it.
