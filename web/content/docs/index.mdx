Vidiyal is a review desk for Bitget. It reads a signed record of what an agent or a person
actually traded, rebuilds the market around every entry, grades the decision on five process
scores with the number behind each, finds the habits that repeat, and turns them into a checklist
the next order has to pass before Bitget would even see it.

Start at the [quick start](/docs/getting-started/quick-start): install, the checks, one review
against a Kaaval ledger, and the desk.
